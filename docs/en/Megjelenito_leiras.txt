VIEWER – GUIDE
==============

Megjelenítő.exe makes charts and statistics from the measurements in the data folder.
Internet is only needed for the Refresh button (to download new data), not for viewing.


REFRESH BUTTON
--------------

Runs the Downloader (new data from Weather Underground), then re-reads the data folder. The header
shows what it is doing. If there is no internet or the API key is missing, the program tells you and
keeps working with the existing data.


TABS
----

  Overview     – "What changed" bar, today's cards, watch bar, sky (sun, moon), weekly summary
  Timeline     – six charts zoomed together, with markers; custom formula band
  Calendar     – calendar heatmap by year or month, garden diary
  Drought      – soil water balance, watering advice, irrigation programs, fire danger (FWI), VPD, heat sum;
                 "Water" sub-tab: water use and water demand with costs
  Rain         – list of rain events and monthly rain
  Wind         – hour × month heatmap, wind rose, windy afternoons
  Statistics   – period selector, records, months compared, hour × day heatmap, average day,
                 periods overlaid, data quality
  Running      – comfort index and a learning running-clothing advisor
  Report       – monthly weather report and yearbook (print / PDF)
  Settings     – appearance, language, font size, units, alerts, automatic download, backup,
                 rain gauge calibration, suspicious measurements, Excel export, folders, change log

The moon icon in the header switches quickly between light and dark theme. The chosen tab, theme and
all view settings are kept (appdata_megjelenítő\felulet.json).


LANGUAGE, UNITS, FONT SIZE
--------------------------

Settings › Language: Hungarian, English, German or Spanish (all text, the charts, the Downloader's
messages and alerts too). A new language can be added with a single file – see FORDITAS.md in the
source code.

Units: temperature °C/°F, rain mm/inch, wind km/h – m/s – knots – mph – Beaufort, pressure hPa/inHg.
Internally the program calculates in metric units; only the display, charts, report and export change.
Beaufort is fractional (WMO formula: B = (v / 0.836)^(2/3), v in m/s); on the Wind tab with 1 decimal,
the average wind with 2.

Minimum font size: Normal, 14, 16 or 18 px (values in between with the slider); small and medium text
grows, large headings hardly. Shortcut: Ctrl + plus / Ctrl + minus (Ctrl + 0: default).


OVERVIEW: WHAT CHANGED, WATCH, SKY
----------------------------------

At the top the "What changed" bar: if you opened the program in the last 24 hours, "Since yesterday",
otherwise "Since the last opening (date)" – rain, fronts, thunderstorms, frost, strong gusts, how
depletion changed and what to do, new records, data gaps. After a longer break it can be expanded
day by day. Clicking an item jumps to it on the timeline. × closes it until the next opening.

Below the "Today" cards the watch bar: frost risk, heat, fast pressure drop, strong wind, heavy rain,
mugginess, lawn water shortage, high fire danger, Zambretti forecast. Next to it sunrise, sunset, day
length, moon phase; "The month's extremes" (with several years of data "On this day").


ƒx – FORMULAS AND ADJUSTABLE NUMBERS
------------------------------------

Next to computed values (feels like, comfort, Beaufort, sunshine, Zambretti, rain events, front and
thunderstorm detector, statistical thresholds, windy afternoons, month-end estimate, running model,
suspicious measurements, evaporation, fire danger, VPD, heat sum) an ƒx icon opens a window: the formula,
an explanation, the parameters with their formula symbols and standard values, a reset button and named
presets. Invalid values cannot be saved (the field turns red and the error is shown below it).


TIMELINE
--------

Six charts: temperature and dew point, humidity, pressure, wind and gusts, rain, solar radiation and
UV. Period buttons: 24 hours · 3 days · 7 days · 30 days · This year · All.
Zoom with the top slider or Ctrl + mouse wheel; pan by dragging.
- Orange dots: suspicious measurements. Grey bands: data gaps.
- Dashed vertical lines: front passage (❄) and thunderstorm (⚡); blue bands: big rains;
  green dotted line: garden diary entry.

CUSTOM FORMULA: at the bottom of the Timeline you can plot your own index, e.g.:
  eső(t) / (napsütés(t-1nap) * légnyomás(t-3nap) * 0.98 - 1)
Variable and function names are Hungarian (the list is shown next to the editor): hom (temperature),
para (humidity), légnyomás (pressure), szél (wind), lökés (gust), eső (rain rate), eső_összeg (rain),
napsütés (solar), uv, vpd, et0, fwi…; time shifts x(t-30perc), x(t-2óra), x(t-1nap); functions
összeg (sum), átlag (average), min, max, abs, gyök (sqrt), ln, log, exp, kerekít (round), ha (if).


CALENDAR AND GARDEN DIARY
-------------------------

Year or month view; the quantity can be chosen and the colour scale follows it. Striped days have
incomplete data. Clicking a day jumps to it on the timeline.
Garden diary: date, tag (pruning, fertilising, spraying, flowering, harvest, watering, planting, other)
and a note. Watering also goes into the soil water balance; flowering dates refine the heat-sum milestones.


DROUGHT, WATERING, WATER
------------------------

From the measured weather the program computes evaporation (FAO-56 Penman–Monteith, ET₀) and keeps a
daily soil water balance. The ? button next to the depletion % explains the number: the root zone is a
bucket, 0% = full, 100% = empty (wilting point); bands from the profile's own stress threshold, Ks, the
bucket size, the current deficit and how many days without rain until the stress threshold.

- Profiles: soil surface, lawn, shrubs, pots, trees; can be turned on/off, new ones added.
- Fire danger (Canadian FWI) with a step-by-step explanation and a "Today's calculation" table (inputs →
  intermediate values → results). On the fire-danger chart rainy days are blue dots (sized by the rain).
- Watering advice for lawn, shrubs, pots and trees.
- Irrigation programs: a program can be saved by name (zone, weekdays or every x days, amount per run in
  minutes, mm or litres). For a new period just choose it and give the from–to dates (or "every year"
  as MM-DD). Record one-off watering on the cards under "I watered…". The balance includes all recorded
  and programmed watering.
- Water sub-tab: from the areas (lawn m², shrubs, canopy) the actual watering in m³ and money, and the
  water demand: how much water would have been needed to keep the plants at the chosen target (target =
  at most this % depletion, per plant with a slider). Water price: one price, or water + sewer fee.
  Tile calendar, monthly bars (demand, watering, rain), summary cards and target comparison.
  The period comes from the Statistics tab's period selector.


RAIN, WIND, STATISTICS
----------------------

Rain: start, length, total and max. intensity of every shower, monthly rain, filters.
Wind: average wind by time of day and month, wind rose, list of afternoons above 3 Bft.
Statistics: at the top the period selector – rows "year. month. day. – year. month. day.", as many as
you like with +; an empty "from" is the first measured day, an empty "to" is today. Quick buttons (each
year, previous 365 days, whole period; Ctrl + click adds), favourites by name. With several periods
"Combined set" or "Side by side" (a column / line per period). The choice applies to the whole tab.
"Periods overlaid": any number of start days, length 1 day / 1 week / 1 month / custom, several data types
(different units go to separate small charts).


RUNNING, REPORT
---------------

Running: comfort index and running-clothing advisor; it learns from 5 values after a run (when,
intensity, layers, feeling, who). Report: monthly weather report and yearbook, printable / PDF.


SETTINGS
--------

- Automatic download: every 15/30/60/180 minutes, custom interval (X minutes/hours/days), daily at a
  set time, or weekly on chosen days (Windows Task Scheduler).
- Backup: frequency, number kept, "Back up now"; the Viewer also checks at startup.
- Rain gauge calibration: any number of "from – to – manual reading" rows; the program computes the
  station's reading for the same period and the factor (Σ manual / Σ station). It reports an error if
  the manual or the station reading is 0 ("is the rain gauge clogged?"), and warns about overlaps and
  data gaps. Global or per-period factor, can be overridden; calibrated rain is used everywhere, when
  turned off the raw value is shown.
- Suspicious measurements: WU flags (qcStatus 0 and −1) and the program's own rules (impossible value,
  spike, overheating due to sunshine, first measurement after an outage) can be switched separately,
  with parameters; the number of flagged measurements is shown immediately.
- Change log: when, what and to what you changed; one click restores the old value.
- Update: if a newer Idojaras_telepito_v*.exe is in the program folder or next to it, a bar offers it.
- Excel export: the daily summary as CSV, in the chosen units.

Security: the programs only write into their own folder (exceptions: the shortcut and the scheduled
task created by the installer, and the Excel export, whose location you choose).

More thresholds: appdata_megjelenítő\megjelenito.ini (most are also adjustable in the ƒx windows).
The cache, lib and WebView2 subfolders of appdata_megjelenítő can be deleted; the program recreates them.


IF SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK
--------------------------

- "No weather data yet": click Download data, or first start the Downloader once and enter the API key.
- The program opens in a browser instead of a window: Microsoft Edge WebView2 is missing (Windows 11
  has it by default). It still works as long as the small "the program is running" window is open.
- Error log: appdata_megjelenítő\megjelenito_hiba.log
